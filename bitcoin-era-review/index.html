Bitcoin Era review: scam warning, and what this site is now

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Short answer. The “Bitcoin Era” auto-trading software advertised online is not regulated by any major authority and has been flagged by a regulator. Do not send it money. This website, bitcoinera.com, now belongs to an independent publisher and runs something else: a free crypto bot challenge with virtual money only.

What Bitcoin Era claimed to be

Bitcoin Era was promoted as an automated trading robot: deposit a few hundred dollars, switch the bot on, and let an algorithm trade crypto for you. The ads promised high daily returns, little effort and no experience needed.

The regulator warning

In December 2020, the Securities Commission Malaysia added Bitcoin Era to its Investor Alert List, describing it as a possible clone carrying on unlicensed capital market activities (source). Independent broker review sites also note that it is not supervised by any top-tier regulator (source).

Red flags to look for in any trading robot

  • Guaranteed or very high returns. No strategy wins every day. Anyone promising it is selling something else.
  • Celebrity endorsements in ads. Fake news pages featuring famous names are a classic hook for these schemes.
  • A deposit before you can test anything. A real tool lets you see it work before your money is involved.
  • Unregulated “partner brokers”. Check the broker on your regulator’s register before sending a cent.
  • Pressure to deposit more. Calls from “account managers” pushing you to top up are a warning sign on their own.
