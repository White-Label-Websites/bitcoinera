Questions

Before you build

Is it free? Do I need a card?

Yes, it is free, and no. Signing up asks for no card, no deposit and no payment of any kind.

Is any real money involved?

No. Every bot starts with a virtual balance of $10,000. Prices come from live markets, the money does not.

What is a drawdown?

A drawdown is how far your balance falls from its starting point. The maximum drawdown is -10% over the whole level, and the daily drawdown is -5% within any 24 hours.

How long does a level take?

Level 1 runs for at least 5 and at most 10 trading days. Level 2 needs at least 5 trading days with no time limit. Level 3 has no minimum duration.

What happens if my bot breaks a rule?

Breaching the daily or the maximum drawdown ends the challenge immediately. You can review what happened, adjust your strategy and start a new bot.
